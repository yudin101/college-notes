## Elasticity Demand (ED)

### Percentage/Proportional Method

$$
\%\Delta Q = \left( \frac{Q_2 - Q_1}{Q_1} \right) \times 100\%
$$

$$
\%\Delta P = \left( \frac{P_2 - P_1}{P_1} \right) \times 100\%
$$

$$
ED = \frac{\%\Delta Q}{\%\Delta P}
$$

### Arc/midpoint Method

$$
\Delta Q = Q_2 - Q_1
$$

$$
\Delta P = P_2 - P_1
$$

$$
ED = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta P} \times \frac{P_1 + P_2}{Q_1 + Q_2}
$$

### Point Method

$$
\Delta Q = Q_2 - Q_1
$$

$$
\Delta P = P_2 - P_1
$$

$$
ED = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta P} \times \frac{P_1}{Q_1}
$$

## Cost (Q, TFC, TC, TVC, AFC, AVC, AC, MC)

### Formulas

- Q = Output
- TFC = Already given, if not, TC at Q = 0
- TC = TFC + TVC
- TVC = TC - TFC
- AFC = $\frac{TFC}{Q}$
- AVC = $\frac{TVC}{Q}$
- {AC/ATC} = $\frac{TC}{Q}$
- MC = current TC - previous TC

### Things to remember

- Use "-" for AFC, AVC, AC, MC at Q = 0
- When MC < {AC/AVC} -> {AC/AVC} falls
- When MC = {AC/AVC} -> {AC/AVC} is at it's minimum
- When MC > {AC/AVC} -> {AC/AVC} rises

## National Income

### Formulas

- Ignore things like `intermediate goods`, `transfer payments`, `savings`, `foreign investments`

#### GDP/GDPmp = Consumption + Investment + Government Expenditure + (Export - Import)

- C + I + G + ( X - M)
- `Consumption` = household consumption, personal consumption, private final consumption
- `Investment` = {gross domestic capital formation, gross private domestic fixed investment} + {change in stock, change in business inventories, closing stock - opening stock}
- `Government` Expenditure = government final consumption expenditure, government consumption and investment expenditure
- Net exports might be given instead of separate exports and imports. In such case, directly use net exports as exports - imports
- `Net exports` = Export - Import

#### GNP/GNI/GNPmp = GDP + net factor income from abroad

#### NNP/NNI/NNPmp = GNP - d

- d = depreciation, consumption of fixed capital, capital consumption allowance

#### National Income = NNP - indirect tax + subsidies

- When net indirect tax or indirect tax less subsidies is given, just subtract it

## Market Equilibrium and Tax

### To calculate equilibrium price and quantity

- Take Qs = Qd -> Find Price (P)
- Take P and replace it in either Qs or Qd and find the Quantity

### To calculate equilibrium after tax / subsidy

#### Tax on seller / buyer

- Tax imposed on seller = Replace Qs' P with (P - tax)
- Tax imposed on buyer = Replace Qd' P with (P + tax)

#### Subsidy on seller / buyer

- Subsidy on seller = Replace Qs' P with (P + subsidy)
- Subsidy on buyer = Replace Qd' P with (P - subsidy)

### Gains / burdens

- Buyer's gain / burden = price buyer pays now vs old equilibrium price
- Seller's gain / burden = price seller receives nw vs old equilibrium price

## Profit maximization and perfect competition

P and C (TC and C are the same thing) will be given. Most probably, both in the form of equations.

- **`Total Revenue (TR)`** = P * Q (multiply P with literally the 'Q' variable)
- **`Marginal Revenue (MR)`** = $\frac{d(TR)}{dQ}$ (derivative)
- **`Marginal Cost (MC)`** = $\frac{d(TC)}{dQ}$ (derivative)
- Equate: **`MR = MC`** -> Find Q
- Put that Q in P's equation from the question and find P's value
- Find TR; **`TR = P * Q`**
- Find TC -> Put Q's value in the question's equation
- **`Profit`** = TR - TC

## Budget line

Things that will be given:

- Income (M)
- Price of X ($P_x$)
- Price of Y ($P_y$)

### Equation

$$
\frac{P_x \times X}{P_y \times Y} = M
$$

### For diagrams

- X intercept:
  - $P_x \times X$ = M
  - Y = 0
- Y intercept:
  - $P_y \times Y$ = M
  - X = 0

### Finding Slope

- Slope: $\frac{-P_x}{P_y}$

### Spending equally on both, how many units bought?

- Money divided for each (D) = $\frac{M}{2}$
- For X: $\frac{D}{P_x}$
- For Y: $\frac{D}{P_y}$
